# 13 — Architecture Decision Record (ADR)

```md
Create an ADR for this decision.

## Decision
<one sentence>

## Context
- Current constraints
- Alternatives considered
- Technical debt impact

## Decision drivers
- Reliability
- Maintainability
- Cost/performance
- Reversibility

## Decision
- Chosen option
- Why this over alternatives
- Tradeoffs accepted

## Consequences
- Positive
- Negative
- Follow-up actions

## Rule
No ADR without at least 2 serious alternatives.
```
